Privacy Policy

First Coast Sales Group, Inc., a Florida corporation ("First Coast Sales Group," "we," "us," or "our"), is an outsourced sales and marketing firm based in Jacksonville, Florida. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information collected through our website at https://www.firstcoastsalesgroup.com and through the phone, email, and SMS communication our recruiting team sends. 01Information We Collect

Information you give us directly

  • Name and email address.
  • Phone number, used to call or text you about your application, where you have consented.
  • Mailing address, if you provide one.
  • Application and resume information, such as work history, education, written responses, and any video responses you submit.
  • Form submissions, including messages you send through our contact form.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, we and our hosting provider may automatically collect your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, the pages you visit, and the dates and times of your visits.

What we don't collect

Our website is informational. We do not sell products, process payments, or provide a subscription or software service through it, so we do not collect payment card or bank information. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information, such as Social Security numbers, health information, or precise geolocation, through the website.

02How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Evaluate your application for our sales and marketing positions.
  • Schedule and coordinate interviews, including sending confirmations and reminders.
  • Send you updates on the status of your application by phone, email, or SMS.
  • Respond to inquiries and questions you send us.
  • Operate, secure, and improve our website.
  • Comply with legal obligations and maintain required records.

We do not use automated decision-making to evaluate candidates. A person on our team reviews every application.

03SMS Communications

First Coast Sales Group, Inc. offers an optional SMS program for candidates. If you enroll, we will send only the following types of messages:

  • Interview scheduling, confirmations, and reminders
  • Updates on the status of your application
  • Replies to questions you send our recruiting team

How you opt in

You are enrolled in our SMS program only when you affirmatively opt in, either by checking a clearly labeled SMS consent checkbox on one of our web forms or by giving us separate express written consent. The SMS consent checkbox is never pre-checked. No one is added to our SMS program from a resume, a referral, a purchased list, or any other source.

No mobile information, phone numbers, or SMS consent data will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This data will not be sold, rented, or disclosed to any third party for their own marketing use under any circumstances.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of employment consideration, of submitting an application, or of being interviewed.

Message frequency, rates, and help

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at contact@firstcoastsalesgroup.com.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop all further messages. You will receive one confirmation message acknowledging your opt-out, and no further texts after that. Opting out of SMS does not withdraw your application; we will continue to contact you about it by phone or email.

Delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

04Email Communications

You can unsubscribe from any promotional email we send by using the unsubscribe link in that email or by emailing contact@firstcoastsalesgroup.com. While your application is active, we may still send you transactional messages about it, such as interview scheduling and status updates. Once your application is closed, you can ask us to stop all contact.

05Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information.

SMS opt-in data, mobile phone numbers, and messaging consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We share personal information only in the following cases:

  • Service providers. Vendors that host our website, deliver our email and SMS messages, and store applicant records, under data processing agreements that limit their use of your information to providing those services to us.
  • Legal compliance. When required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when needed to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.
  • Internal staff and contractors. Members of our team and contractors who need the information to review applications and coordinate hiring.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our business, in which case your information remains subject to this policy.

06Data Retention & Security

We keep your information for as long as needed to consider your candidacy and to meet our legal recordkeeping obligations. If you are hired, your application information becomes part of your employment record. If you are not hired, we may retain your information to consider you for future openings, and we will delete it if you ask us to.

We protect your information with access controls, encryption in transit, and vetted vendors. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by Florida and federal law.

07Cookies & Tracking

We use a minimal set of cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, remember your preferences, and understand site usage through aggregated analytics. We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, though parts of the site may not work as intended. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals sent by your browser.

08Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of SMS, email, or other communications.
  • Port your information to you in a portable format.
  • Restrict how we use your information.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@firstcoastsalesgroup.com with your name and the email address you used on your application. We will verify your identity before acting on the request and respond within 45 days. Exercising your rights will not affect how we evaluate your application.

09California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), gives you the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; to request deletion or correction of your personal information; to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information; to limit the use of sensitive personal information; and to be free from discrimination or retaliation for exercising these rights.

We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined by the CCPA. You can submit a request at no charge by emailing contact@firstcoastsalesgroup.com.

10Age Restrictions

Our website and application process are intended for people 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it.

11Changes to This Policy

When we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. If we make a material change, including any change to our SMS program, we will notify you by email or SMS before the change takes effect.
